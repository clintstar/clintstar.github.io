# clintstar.github.io

Empowering African agriculture with a multi-vendor marketplace that connects smallholder farmers and cooperatives directly with buyers. We prioritize direct markets and supply-chain transparency, with digital payments (Pi integration) as the finance layer — no lending or credit features.

Core focus:
- Farmer & cooperative profiles and pooled listings
- Product catalog with quality grading and pre-harvest offtake support
- Price discovery board and transaction tracking
- Supply chain traceability (batch/lot records)
- Offline-friendly, low-literacy UI with local language support
